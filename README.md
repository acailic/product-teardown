# product-teardown

Daily teardowns of small businesses and successful products: **how it earns, who the customers are, why they use it, and what could be built better.**

One analysis per day. Each teardown lives in `teardowns/YYYY-MM-DD-<business>.md` and answers the same five questions with public evidence. Methodology: [`docs/METHOD.md`](docs/METHOD.md) — template: [`teardowns/template.md`](teardowns/template.md).

## Why

Learning how successful products are actually made — revenue mechanics, customer motivations, and the gap between what a product does and what a better version could do.

## Method

Every teardown answers: (1) what the product is and for whom, (2) how it earns — model, prices, revenue units, visible evidence, (3) who the customers are — segments with public evidence, (4) why they use it — job-to-be-done and the realistic alternatives, (5) what we would build better — 1–3 concrete, argued changes. Public sources only; every non-trivial claim carries a link. Full method: [`docs/METHOD.md`](docs/METHOD.md).
