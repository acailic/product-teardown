# Carrd — how a $19/year tool built a million-dollar business

*Teardown date: 2026-11-05. Pricing verified on [carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro) in November 2026; revenue figures are dated founder statements, linked inline.*

## The essence (for a 9-year-old)

Carrd is a machine for making one small page on the internet — like a single poster, not a whole book. You pick a ready-made poster, drop in your words and pictures, and it's live in minutes, without even creating an account first. The basic poster is free, but if you want it to say your own name (like `yourname.com`) and drop the tiny "Made with Carrd" stamp, you pay a little: $19 for a whole year, about the price of one pizza. Millions of people paying a little bit each turned one programmer and his friend into a million-dollar-a-year company.

## What the product is, and who it's for

Carrd is a browser-based builder for **simple, responsive, one-page sites** — personal profiles, landing pages that collect emails, portfolios, link-in-bio pages ([carrd.co](https://carrd.co/)). It launched in March 2016 via Twitter and Product Hunt, built solo by AJ, the designer behind the free-template site [HTML5 UP](https://themakingof.carrd.co/). Of four niches he considered, he picked **profile** because it was one-page simple, appealed to almost anyone, and his one-page templates were his most downloaded ([The Making Of](https://themakingof.carrd.co/)). The signature move is friction-free onboarding: start building at `carrd.co/build` with **no signup required** ([The Making Of](https://themakingof.carrd.co/); AJ credits this for his Product Hunt wave in the [2021 AMA](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hi-im-aj-maker-of-carrd-and-after-2-5m-sites-1m-arr-and-a-funding-round-probably-time-for-an-ama-1b7fcf52fe)).

## How it earns money (model, prices, revenue unit)

**Freemium** — paid plans "automatically renew yearly or simply pay as you go" ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)); pricing dial is *number of sites per account*:

- **Free**: 3 sites on a `*.carrd.co` URL with "Made with Carrd" branding ([carrd.co](https://carrd.co/)).
- **Pro Lite** from **$9/year**: removes branding, premium templates — no custom domains or forms ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)).
- **Pro Standard** from **$19/year** (10 sites): custom domains, forms wired to 20 email services, embeds (Stripe, Gumroad), analytics; scales to **$599/year for 1,000 sites** ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)).
- **Pro Plus** from **$49/year** (25 sites): adds advanced forms, password protection, and site downloads/export ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)).

A 7-day Pro trial requires no credit card ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)). Yearly billing was launch philosophy: "the last thing anyone wanted was yet another monthly service" ([The Making Of](https://themakingof.carrd.co/)) — plans page now offers pay-as-you-go. **Revenue unit: one annual subscription per account.** Founder-confirmed milestones: **>$1M ARR** with 2.5M sites and ~10k new sites/day (April 2021 [AMA](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hi-im-aj-maker-of-carrd-and-after-2-5m-sites-1m-arr-and-a-funding-round-probably-time-for-an-ama-1b7fcf52fe)); later **~$100K MRR (~$1.2M ARR)**, 4M sites, still profitable after a **$2M funding round** ([SaaS Club #306](https://saasclub.io/podcast/carrd-aj-306/)). **Current revenue: unknown** — not publicly disclosed; a "~$2M ARR" figure from a MicroConf talk is creator-reported, not founder-confirmed ([ProvenStartups](https://provenstartups.com/blog/revenue-reality/carrd-revenue)).

## Who the customers are

- **Solo founders validating ideas** — a landing page with a signup form before writing code; one HN user launched a marketplace with "Just Carrd, Google Form and Reddit" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44388271)).
- **Job seekers and freelancers** using it as a résumé/portfolio page (e.g. `druvith-gowda.carrd.co` in HN's Who-is-hiring threads — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41728419)).
- **Creators and Gen-Z web culture** — "Carrd is ubiquitous among a certain kind of zoomer… the closest analogy to Tumblr customization Gen Z has" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31670904)); fandom and activism sites like `50501.carrd.co` spread the same way.
- **Small businesses and agencies** — proven by the ladder of 50/100/250/500/1000-site tiers; nobody needs 1,000 sites for personal use ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)).
- **Who pays**: "more business-oriented use cases went Pro, more personal-oriented generally didn't — but lately it seems to be happening across all use cases" — AJ, [2021 AMA](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hi-im-aj-maker-of-carrd-and-after-2-5m-sites-1m-arr-and-a-funding-round-probably-time-for-an-ama-1b7fcf52fe).

## Why they use it instead of alternatives

The job-to-be-done: **"put a decent-looking page on the internet today, without learning anything, for almost nothing."**

- vs **Squarespace/Wix**: monthly-priced, feature-rich builders; HN users summarize the split as "just want a website for cheap: Bearblog, carrd.co… all the bells and whistles on a platter: Squarespace, Wix" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48710640)). Carrd is the one-decision version.
- vs **WordPress/GitHub Pages**: no hosting, plugins, or code required (signal: search-sourced).
- vs **Linktree-style bio tools**: a Carrd page is a *real* page — backgrounds, text, forms, payments — adopted as an "extended profile" on social platforms (AJ, [2021 AMA](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hi-im-aj-maker-of-carrd-and-after-2-5m-sites-1m-arr-and-a-funding-round-probably-time-for-an-ama-1b7fcf52fe)).
- The one-page constraint was scarcity turned into positioning: AJ "couldn't build a Squarespace so I had to reduce my scope, which ended up being one of Carrd's biggest differentiators" ([AMA](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hi-im-aj-maker-of-carrd-and-after-2-5m-sites-1m-arr-and-a-funding-round-probably-time-for-an-ama-1b7fcf52fe)). Free sites carry "Made with Carrd", so **free users are the marketing** — AJ credits the community, not paid acquisition, for growth to $30K MRR with "zero marketing" ([SaaS Club](https://saasclub.io/podcast/carrd-aj-306/); [saashacker summary](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=21333522)).

## What I would build better

1. **Make export/ownership nearly free.** "Download Sites" is locked in the top Pro Plus tier ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)), and people who outgrow Carrd rip it out entirely — there's a public [Carrd → Cloudflare Pages migration guide](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37620754). Cheap export would trade a little lock-in for graduation trust — and keep the $19 customer longer.
2. **Own the "graduation moment" with a lightweight content layer.** The most-cited reason to leave is needing a blog/multi-page/SEO: users pair Carrd for the landing with Feather for the blog ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33145855)), and challengers market themselves on exactly that gap — "PulsePages – multi-page websites for $9/year (Carrd alternative)" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48021193)). A native, one-page-simple blog mode would close the top leak.
3. **Team workflows for the 1,000-site customer.** The site-count ladder proves an agency/studio segment ([carrd.com/pro](https://carrd.com/pro)), but Carrd is single-player: no client roles, review links, or whitelabel dashboards. For studios, collaboration — not page count — is the top-of-funnel feature.
