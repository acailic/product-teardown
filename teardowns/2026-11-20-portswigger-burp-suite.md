# Teardown: PortSwigger (Burp Suite) — the free tool that became the profession's language

- **Date analyzed:** 2026-11-20
- **Category:** B2B/B2C · desktop toolkit + SaaS (application security testing)
- **Model:** per-user annual licensing (Professional), quote-based organization subscriptions (DAST), usage-metered AI add-on (Burp AT), one-off exam credits (certification); Community Edition and Web Security Academy free
- **Prices as seen (2026-11-20):** Burp Suite Professional **$499/user/year** (€475, £419; 1–10-year terms, every user needs a licence — [buy form](https://portswigger.net/buy/pro)) · Burp AT (public beta) $19 / $89 / $249 per user/month by usage, small allowance bundled with Pro ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/burp-at/pricing)) · Burp Suite Certified Practitioner exam **$99**, requires an active Pro subscription ([certification](https://portswigger.net/web-security/certification)) · Burp Suite DAST quote-only — "tailored" subscriptions, unlimited users, metered "from ad-hoc scanning managed by the hour to unlimited" ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast/pricing))
- **Revenue evidence:** **estimated** — private company, no published figures. Verified anchors: $112M minority investment from Brighton Park Capital, first outside money in 16 years ([founder blog, 2024-06-27](https://portswigger.net/blog/investing-to-deliver-more)); "about 20,000 customers" at that date ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/portswigger-scores-hefty-112-million-investment/)); 2019: 40 employees, 15,000 customers in 130 countries ([press release](https://portswigger.net/press-releases/portswigger-honored-with-queens-award-for-enterprise))

## 0. The essence (ELI9)

Burp Suite is the toolbox almost every professional website-hacker works in: it sits between the hacker's browser and the target site, letting them see, replay, and change every message. It began as one Englishman's free hobby project; today tens of thousands of organizations — Microsoft, Amazon, NASA — pay for it, at roughly $500 per hacker per year. The money keeps coming because Burp became the language of the job itself: the training courses teach it, its free school trains the next generation of hackers on it, and its research team keeps discovering the new attack tricks and baking them into the product. The founder owned the whole company himself for sixteen years, then sold a small slice for $112 million.

## 1. What it is, and for whom

Burp Suite is a web-application penetration-testing toolkit: an intercepting proxy plus manual attack tools (Repeater, Intruder, Decoder) and an automated scanner, extensible via the community BApp store ([Pro page](https://portswigger.net/burp/pro)). It ships as a free Community Edition, per-user Professional, an enterprise scanner now rebranded Burp Suite DAST ([DAST page](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast)), and the agentic-AI layer Burp AT ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/burp-at/pricing)). The primary user is the hands-on web security tester; above them sit pentest consultancies and enterprise AppSec teams. PortSwigger Ltd is Knutsford, UK, incorporated 9 October 2008 ([Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/06719143)). Dafydd Stuttard wrote the first Burp in 2003–06 as a hobby, published the Web Application Hacker's Handbook in 2007 (100k+ readers), and only then formed the company ([About](https://portswigger.net/about)). The early-story lesson that earns this larger subject its slot: **give the tool and the textbook away, become the profession's default, then charge per practitioner.**

## 2. How it earns

**Four stacked revenue units** ([About](https://portswigger.net/about), prices seen 2026-11-20):

- **Per-seat licences.** Professional costs $499/user/year (€475/£419) with terms up to 10 years, and "everyone who uses Burp Suite Professional needs a subscription" ([buy form](https://portswigger.net/buy/pro)). The price has climbed from $299, "held steady… for over three years" as of 2015 ([blog](https://portswigger.net/blog/burp-suite-pro-price-held-for-2015)).
- **Quote-gated organization subscriptions.** DAST pricing is fully gated — "tailored to your needs," unlimited users, metered from hourly ad-hoc scans to unlimited scale ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast/pricing)).
- **Usage-metered AI.** Burp AT's public beta sells monthly usage tiers ($19/$89/$249 per user) on top of the licence, with a small allowance bundled into Pro to seed adoption ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/burp-at/pricing)).
- **Exam credits.** The BSCP certification costs $99 per exam — and requires an active Pro subscription to even sit it ([certification](https://portswigger.net/web-security/certification)).

**Revenue: estimated.** No figures are published. The vendor claims "70,000+ customers, including 16,000 enterprises" ([About](https://portswigger.net/about), 2021-era); DAST's page now says "18,000+ organisations across 170+ countries" ([DAST](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast)). At $499/seat plus five-figure enterprise scanner contracts, that base implies a business well past $100M/year — *derived, not stated*. The verified money event is the June 2024 $112M Brighton Park minority investment ([founder blog](https://portswigger.net/blog/investing-to-deliver-more)), landing with "about 20,000 customers" ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/portswigger-scores-hefty-112-million-investment/)).

## 3. Who the customers are

- **Professional pentesters and bug bounty hunters** — the per-seat base. "At Microsoft, Burp Suite is what you use. It's not up for consideration" (vendor-quoted customer, [Pro page](https://portswigger.net/burp/pro)); "I've never run into a web app pentester who didn't know Burp" ([Pro page](https://portswigger.net/burp/pro)).
- **Pentest consultancies and security teams** — multi-seat Pro plus a reseller channel ([resellers](https://portswigger.net/support/reseller-faqs)); BSCP exists so firms can "prove the proficiency of your testing team to potential clients" ([certification](https://portswigger.net/web-security/certification)).
- **Enterprise AppSec/DevSecOps divisions** — DAST buyers: Amazon, Microsoft, NASA, SAP, Salesforce logos; "Customers' Choice" on Gartner Peer Insights; in Gartner's Magic Quadrant since 2011–14 ([DAST](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast), [About](https://portswigger.net/about)) — all vendor claims.
- **Learners** — the Web Security Academy's 200+ free topics hit one million users within a year of launch ([About](https://portswigger.net/about)); they pay nothing today and are the funnel for everything above.

## 4. Why they use it

The job: **find the vulnerabilities a checkbox scanner misses, exploit them by hand, and produce the evidence.** Burp is the workbench where that workflow lives — and PortSwigger keeps loading it: James Kettle's research (server-side template injection, request smuggling) ships as product capability, the BApp store adds 300+ extensions, and the Academy teaches the exact toolchain ([About](https://portswigger.net/about), [Pro page](https://portswigger.net/burp/pro)). Alternatives: OWASP ZAP (free, but "only work[s] well… in combination with manual methods" — [HN, 2025-04-02](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43563103)); the lightweight challenger Caido ("pretty lightweight and can be ran in headless mode… you can run it on a VPS… by design, contrary to my beloved burp/zap" — [HN, 2025-12-20](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46335372)); template scanners like Nuclei for known CVEs. Burp wins on being the profession's shared language: hiring, training, certification, and extensions all assume it — a switching cost no rival has matched, and one Caido's team frames as the race ("catching up very fast… Most of the Burp new features of the last 2 years are basically copying what we innovated in Caido" — [HN, 2025-12-20](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46336497)).

## 5. What I would build better

1. **Rebuild the core as a fast, remote-first service.** The desktop client's weight and UI are the one recurring complaint even loyalists voice — "Burp Pro is my daily driver at work… [Caido's] UI/UX is about as bad as Burp's (which isn't great). The speed is an awesome gain" ([HN, 2025-03-30](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43525546)), and Caido's whole pitch is headless/VPS-native proxying ([caido.io](https://caido.io)). A cloud-resident project with a thin client — multiplayer sessions, shared site maps, scan agents you leave running — defends the $499 seat before the challenger matures, and is the natural substrate for the per-usage Burp AT metering it already sells.
2. **Publish a self-serve team tier.** Between $499-per-user Pro and quote-gated DAST there is a dead zone for the 5–50-person pentest shop: DAST's page says "tailored" and "get in touch" ([pricing](https://portswigger.net/burp/dast/pricing)). A priced team plan (pooled scanning, shared projects, simple SSO) would harvest the mid-market with zero sales touches — the same demo-gate tax this series watched Vanta leave open ([sibling teardown](2026-11-12-vanta.md)).
3. **Sell the funnel's graduates an indie tier.** The Academy trains millions for free, but Community Edition is deliberately hobbled (no scanner, rate-limited Intruder), the $99 exam demands an active Pro licence ([certification](https://portswigger.net/web-security/certification)), and Caido's free tier already wins the graduate on the one thing Burp's doesn't — saving projects for free ([HN, 2025-03-30](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43525546)). A ~$149/year solo tier for bug bounty hunters and students would convert learners who today graduate to Caido or nothing, and seed the next decade of per-seat renewals.
