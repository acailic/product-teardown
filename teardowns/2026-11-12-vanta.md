# Teardown: Vanta — the demo-gated machine that turned SOC 2 into rent

- **Date analyzed:** 2026-11-12
- **Category:** B2B · trust-management / compliance-automation SaaS
- **Model:** annual subscription, demo-gated — tiers by product breadth, with in-tier metering (e.g. questionnaires per year)
- **Prices as seen (2026-11-12):** **none published.** [vanta.com/pricing](https://www.vanta.com/pricing) (HTTP 200) shows four plan cards — Essentials, Plus, Professional ("most popular"), Enterprise — and not one dollar figure; the ask is "Request a free demo today to discuss your business needs and get personalized pricing." Rival Secureframe published a floor the same day: "Fundamentals, Starting at $7,500/year" ([secureframe.com/pricing](https://secureframe.com/pricing))
- **Revenue evidence:** **verified $300M+ ARR** ([Fortune, 2026-04-29](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)); surpassed $100M ARR in FY ending January 2024 ([TechCrunch, 2024-07-24](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)); ≈$19k average per customer ($300M ÷ 16,000) — *derived, not stated*

## 0. The essence (ELI9)

A software company must show big customers a certificate — SOC 2 — proving it handles data safely. Winning it means keeping a giant binder of proof: who approved what, who can touch which data, when things were checked. Filling that binder by hand takes weeks of screenshots. Vanta plugs into the company's tools — GitHub, AWS, employee laptops — and fills the binder by itself, every day, so the yearly inspection is easy. Companies pay a large yearly rent — the price only appears after a demo call — and keep paying because big customers won't sign deals without a fresh binder. About 16,000 companies pay — over $300 million a year.

## 1. What it is, and for whom

Vanta is a "trust management platform": continuous monitoring of security controls against frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, ISO 42001…), automated evidence collection and policies, plus outward-facing products — public Trust Center pages, AI questionnaire automation, third-party risk management, access reviews, AI governance, "Vanta AI" agents — wired to 400+ integrations ([pricing page](https://www.vanta.com/pricing), seen 2026-11-12). The primary user: the founder or security lead at a tech company that must pass customer security reviews to close B2B revenue. Founded 2018 out of Y Combinator (W18) by Christina Cacioppo and Erik Goldman; ~1,000 employees ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)).

## 2. How it earns

Demo-gated annual subscription — the meter is visible, the money is not: Plus includes "Questionnaire Automation (25 questionnaires per year)", Professional raises it to 144 ([pricing page](https://www.vanta.com/pricing), seen 2026-11-12). Verified revenue arc: ARR passed $100M in FY ending January 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)), then crossed $300M by April 2026 — "a tripling of ARR in two years," with ~60% YoY customer growth accelerating four straight quarters and NRR "over 100 percent" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)). Capital path: $50M Series A from Sequoia at ~$500M valuation (2021, [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2021/05/04/vanta-goes-from-low-profile-startup-to-500-million-valuation-with-50-million-sequoia-series-a/), [via HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=27042887)), $40M Series B at $1.6B (2022, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/12/vanta-lands-40m-to-automate-cybersecurity-compliance/)), $150M Series C at $2.45B (2024, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)), $150M Series D at $4.15B led by Wellington (2025, [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)) — over $500M raised. Third-party price points for the category: platforms "cost 10k/year" ([HN, 2026-08-22](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398275)); Drata "expensive though, around 17k" ([HN, 2023-05-04](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35811360)); one buyer found Vanta plus a partnered auditor cheaper than the auditor alone ([HN, 2021-01-17](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=25812013)).

## 3. Who the customers are

- **YC-stage startups chasing a first SOC 2** — "three-quarters of the current YC cohort uses Vanta" ([TechCrunch, 2024-07](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)); first-person vendor timeline: 250+ customers May 2020 ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=23047014)), "over a thousand" April 2021 ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26664496)).
- **Scaled mid-market and public tech** — named customers include Snowflake, Atlassian, Duolingo, Ramp, Cursor, Harvey ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)); public case studies cover Clay, Pendo, Synthesia, Eight Sleep and a law firm ([vanta.com/customers](https://www.vanta.com/customers), seen 2026-11-12).
- **AI-native companies** — Cursor and Harvey are customers as "shadow AI" drives new demand ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)).
- **Non-software data holders** — Omni Hotels uses vendor-risk tooling without building software ([Cacioppo, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)).

## 4. Why they use it

The job is unblocking enterprise revenue: security questionnaires and audit demands stall B2B deals, and Cacioppo frames the product as exactly that trade — "if you can give people credit — which really means revenue — for showing off all the good security work they've done, they will do more good security work" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/trust-management-platform-vanta-raises-150m-series-c-now-valued-at-2-45b/)). Alternatives: manual prep ("a huge pain grabbing screenshots for what feels like a very performative process" — [HN, 2026-08-04](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49172683)); consultants and auditors alone (the full stack runs "$15k+ for the platform, $20k+ for consultants, $15k+ for auditors" — [HN, 2025-12-18](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46310901)); rivals Drata and Secureframe; or doing nothing and losing the deal. Vanta wins on integration depth and evidence pre-organized to shorten auditor work ([HN, 2021-01-17](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=25812013)). The growth kicker is a new inspection event: 70% of companies now hold unsanctioned "shadow AI," with Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor the most-reinstalled offenders ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)) — and Vanta just agreed to acquire runtime-AI-security startup Pillar (announced 2026-11-09, [vanta.com](https://www.vanta.com/resources/vanta-acquires-pillar)).

## 5. What I would build better

1. **Publish the floor price.** The demo gate hides a five-figure bill until the call, and the small-team funnel visibly dies there: "too many small teams get excited about SOC 2, then ghost once they see the total cost" ([HN, 2025-12-18](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46310901)); founders already sell "Vanta/Drata for solo devs" against exactly this ([HN, 2025-05-20](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44038384)), and Secureframe publishes $7,500 while Vanta publishes nothing (both seen 2026-11-12). A priced Essentials tier would harvest the long tail Vanta's YC-cohort dominance creates — and starve the thin-layer wedge ([EvidentTrail teardown, 2026-11-10](2026-11-10-evidenttrail.md)) of its best argument.
2. **Open the trust layer to buyers.** The scarce good is proof, and today SOC 2 reports are private and "basically always gated behind a sales touchpoint" ([HN, 2026-03-22](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47482302)). Vanta already hosts thousands of customer Trust Centers; a buyer-side login verifying any vendor in one place would turn every daily security review into distribution — a two-sided network no evidence-pack sidecar can copy.
3. **Sell the AI event, not the certificate.** SOC 2 has no deadline; AI adoption does — an employee installs Claude today, and security discovers it months later ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/exclusive-vanta-arr-300-million-sequoia-shadow-ai-claude-cursor/)). With Pillar signed days ago ([vanta.com](https://www.vanta.com/resources/vanta-acquires-pillar)), the sharper entry SKU is "find and govern your shadow AI in a week" — a fresh, recurring buying event above the increasingly commodity certificate.
